## Verdict

**2x4 budget — go/no-go.** The full instrument at its worst case (8 voices, COLOR 4-note on both parts, all FX on, high diffusion, echo at max) costs **105 % of the block budget offline**, and **105 % measured inside a real audio callback**. The anchored figure is the one that decides. **Conclusion: the 2x4 architecture does not fit.** The anchored figure is over 100 % of the block budget, so the design has to shed voices or FX.
