## M5j — BODY, measured against the estimate

**`body_2x4` — the figure the milestone is gated on.** Average 295 078, maximum
295 724 cycles: **30.7 % / 30.8 %** of the block. The spec predicted 280 000 –
311 000 cycles (29 – 32 %). **The estimate held**, mid-window. For scale,
`synth_2x4` costs 338 694 in the same run — a BODY deck pair is cheaper than a
SYNTH deck pair, which is what one voice per deck against four buys.

**`body_2x4_string` does not work as an ablation, and that is a result rather
than a fault.** It measures 295 220 against `body_2x4`'s 295 078 — a 0.05 %
difference, which is noise. The plan describes it as "the ablation that prices
the bank in context", but `BodyVoice::process()` runs the Karplus pair **and**
the 24-mode bank on every sample and MATL only weights their blend (spec §3),
so both rows price the same work. The two checksums do differ (`58715fcf` vs
`c4951320`), which confirms MATL genuinely reached the engine and the row is
configured correctly. Isolating the bank's in-context cost would need a build
that compiles one structure out, not a parameter change. The component rows
`body/mode_bank_24` and `body/ks_string_pair_port` already give that split.

**`inst_body_worst` — 900 266 average (93.8 %), 980 090 maximum (102.1 %).**
The plan says to apply the Task 4 voice ladder if this row passes 100 % on its
maximum. It was **not** applied, deliberately. `BodyEngine::kVoices` is already
1, so there is no rung below the current one; and the identically configured
SYNTH row `instrument_worst` measures 978 548 maximum (101.9 %) in the same
run. The two are level. The overshoot is the shared FX chain's, not BODY's,
and it exists on `main` without any BODY deck in the patch.

## Why the verdict above flipped to "does not fit"

The generated verdict reads 102 % where the previous body capture
(`2026-07-26-f58644f-body.md`) read 97 %. This is **not** the BODY engine. It
is the Task 9 tape tap, which runs per sample in `PartFx::process` on every
deck that has FLUX or GRIT engaged — including decks with no BODY on them.

Measured on this Seed by mutation (identical build and profile, tap block
removed, tree restored afterwards):

| row | with tap | without tap | delta | per sample |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `fx_flux_sdram` | 81 143 | 65 168 | −15 975 (−19.7 %) | 166 cyc |
| `fx_grit` | 49 095 | 45 791 | −3 304 (−6.7 %) | 34 cyc |
| `instrument_worst` | 928 201 | 876 594 | −51 607 (−5.6 %) | — |
| `fx_none` | 24 414 | 24 413 | 0 | 0 |

`fx_none` not moving is the attribution: the tap sits inside the
"some FX block engaged" branch, so a deck with no FX pays nothing. Every other
checksum is unchanged; only `inst_body_worst`'s moves, as it must when BODY
loses the tape source. Both mutated figures land back on the pre-branch
capture's numbers (65 344 and 45 817), which rules out cross-build cache
layout as the explanation.

**51 607 cycles is 5.4 % of the block, paid by every patch.** It is what
carries `instrument_worst` from 96.9 % to 101.9 % on its maximum. Task 10
estimated ~40 cycles/sample for this; the FLUX case measures 166.

**Deferred by decision, not by oversight.** FLUX is being redesigned as a BBD
delay (`docs/superpowers/specs/…-flux-bbd-…`), which will replace this code
path and be benched fresh. Gating the tap at control rate — skipping the block
unless some part actually consumes the tape source — is the obvious fix and
remains open for whoever does that work.

The tables below retain full measured precision.*
