# Seed CPU Hunt Victory Image

## Purpose

Create a celebratory illustration for Spotykach's successful CPU hunt on the
Daisy Seed. The image should feel energetic, humorous, and recognizably tied to
the instrument rather than showing a generic football mascot.

## Visual design

- Use a polished, dynamic comic-illustration style.
- Anthropomorphize a Daisy Seed circuit board as a fit football player.
- Show the Seed performing an impressive around-the-world ball trick.
- Integrate Deck A and Deck B as recognizable athletic companions or equipment.
- Visualize the BBD as a glowing bucket-brigade echo trail following the ball.
- Represent BODY, WAVE, SYNTH, and SAMPLER as compact functional emblems on the
  player's kit.
- Suggest FORM/SONG through a tactical play diagram.
- Suggest FLUX, GRIT, and ROOM through distinct energy, texture, and spatial
  effects around the action.
- Include a stadium scoreboard reading `CPU 94%` to communicate the
  under-100-percent victory clearly.

## Composition

Use a landscape sports-poster composition with the Seed as the dominant
full-body subject. Keep the football trick readable, use strong diagonal motion,
and place secondary functions around the main action without turning the image
into a dense technical diagram.

## Constraints

- No real-world team branding, sponsor logos, or unrelated characters.
- Keep all visible labels short, uppercase, and legible.
- The Seed must still read as a circuit board despite its human pose.
- The mood is triumphant and playful, not aggressive.
- No watermark.

## Success criteria

At first glance, the image reads as a football victory scene. At second glance,
Spotykach contributors recognize the Daisy Seed, two decks, BBD, the four sound
engines, FORM/SONG, and the CPU result.
