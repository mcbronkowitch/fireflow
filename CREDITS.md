### FLUX's bucket-brigade delay (2026-07-27 redesign)

Holters & Parker, *A Combined Model for a Bucket Brigade Device and its Input
and Output Filters* (DAFx-18) - the combined BBD-and-filters model FLUX's
delay is built on.

Raffel & Smith, *Practical Modeling of Bucket-Brigade Device Circuits*
(DAFx-10) - the compander, nonlinearity and filter-block modeling behind the
same redesign.

[jpcima](https://github.com/jpcima) - [bbd-delay-experimental](https://github.com/jpcima/bbd-delay-experimental),
the reference implementation FLUX's `engine/fx/bbd.{h,cpp}` is ported from
(Boost Software License 1.0; full attribution in `THIRD_PARTY.md`).
