## 3. The Eurorack connector -- the one thing here that is NOT from a datasheet

## 5. Known ERC violations, and why they stay

All three come from the vendored Patch SM symbol naming pins after their default peripheral while the coupon uses them for something else -- the trap recorded in `hardware/lib/README.md`. Fixing them would mean editing a vendored symbol, which would break its provenance for a cosmetic gain.

- `U_SR1.SER` reported as not driven: `B7` is `I2C1_SCL` in the symbol, not declared an output.
- `U_IN1.Q7` against `U_SM.D10`: two outputs connected, because `D10` is `SPI_SCK` in the symbol while the coupon reads it as the chain's data input.
- `lib_symbol_mismatch` on the 165: the embedded copy has its unit sub-symbols renamed from `74LS165_*` to `74HC165_*`, which is what makes a derived symbol loadable at all.
